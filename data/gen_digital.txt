============================================================
GEN DIGITAL - COMPANY OVERVIEW
============================================================

Gen Digital is a global company dedicated to powering digital freedom through its trusted consumer brands, including Avast, AVG, CCleaner, Norton, LifeLock and MoneyLion.

Core Mission: Deliver award-winning cybersecurity, online privacy, identity protection and financial wellness solutions to consumers worldwide.

User Base: Nearly 500 million users in more than 150 countries.
Company Type: Global consumer SaaS security company
Key Brands: Avast, AVG, CCleaner, Norton, LifeLock, MoneyLion

============================================================
PRODUCTS AND BRANDS
============================================================

--- Avast ---

Avast is a leading cybersecurity product providing free and premium antivirus and internet security solutions. It is one of Gen Digital's core freemium products, offering protection against malware, ransomware, phishing, and other online threats.

--- AVG ---

AVG is a trusted cybersecurity brand offering antivirus, internet security, and performance optimization tools. Like Avast, AVG operates on a freemium model, bringing users into the ecosystem through free products and converting them into paying customers through smart in-product journeys.

--- CCleaner ---

CCleaner is a widely used system optimization and cleaning tool that helps users improve the performance and privacy of their devices. It is another core freemium product in Gen Digital's portfolio, with upsell and cross-sell opportunities.

--- Norton ---

Norton is a premium cybersecurity brand providing comprehensive protection against online threats including viruses, malware, ransomware, and identity theft. Norton offers advanced security features for individuals and families.

--- LifeLock ---

LifeLock is an identity theft protection service that monitors personal information and alerts users to potential threats. It provides identity restoration support and financial protection.

--- MoneyLion ---

MoneyLion is a financial wellness platform providing solutions for personal finance management, banking, and financial health.

============================================================
BUSINESS MODEL - FREEMIUM GROWTH
============================================================

Gen Digital operates a freemium business model across its key brands (Avast, AVG, CCleaner):

Growth Strategy:
- Bring people into free products (Avast, AVG, CCleaner)
- Convert the right users into happy paying customers
- Smart in-product journeys for upsell and cross-sell
- Data-driven experimentation to improve conversion and revenue

The Freemium Growth team works within the Customer Success Management (CSM) organisation and focuses on:
- User acquisition into free products
- Conversion optimization from free to paid
- In-product experience improvements
- Cross-sell and upsell strategies

The team works closely with Product, Engineering, Data and CSM departments and is building AI-driven ways of working to move faster and run more experiments.
